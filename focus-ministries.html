InterVarsity Focus Ministries

Black Campus Ministry (BCM)

BCM is a space for students to explore their relationship with Jesus as it relates to their black identity, while engaging in meaningful community.

Asian InterVarsity (AIV)

Asian InterVarsity is a Christ-centered community of Asian students built upon radical love and the Word of God. We welcome students of any background and at any point of their faith journey. As a group we host large groups, bible studies, discipleship groups, worship events, meals, and social events to create space for growth, connection, prayer, worship, and fellowship. Overall, our goal is to live out and grow our faith together.

South Asian InterVarsity (SAIV)

South Asian InterVarsity is a space where South Asian students can experience deep community and explore the intersectionality between their culture and their faith.

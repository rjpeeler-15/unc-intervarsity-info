Large Group at InterVarsity

The What

Large Group is chapter-wide gathering where we come together as a community to worship, hear the Word, play games, and fellowship together. Our main focus is to provide a space for the campus to come together and worship God. These gatherings are an amazing chance to connect with others in the chapter and to engage with God. Once a month we host a community night in place of Large Group, where the chapter gathers for fun events, service opportunities, and more.

The Why

We want everyone on our campus to have the opportunity to behold the glory of God. Large Group exists because we believe that worshipping and glorifying God together is of utmost importance. Small Groups are at the core of InterVarsity, but we also believe in coming together as a chapter for fun and fellowship. Large Group is a great way to get connected with the rest of the chapter and see how God is moving all across our campus!

The When

Large Group meets weekly on Fridays at 7pm, but there are often pre and post Large Group hangouts. Large Group is the perfect way to kick off your weekend! Keep an eye on our GroupMe or Instagram (linked below) for more information and locations!
